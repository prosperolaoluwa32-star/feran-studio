Pricing questions

Straight answers.

How much does a website cost?

At Feran Studio, a small business website starts at $1,200 for up to 5 pages. A custom-designed business website starts at $2,800, and a premium site with animation and a CMS starts at $5,500. Every project gets a fixed quote after a free consultation.

How much does a Shopify store cost?

A Shopify store starts at $2,500 with your brand, up to 25 products, payments and shipping set up. Fully custom stores start at $6,000.

How much does it cost to build a mobile app?

A first version (MVP) for iOS and Android starts at $9,000, including App Store and Google Play submission. Full products with accounts, a backend and AI features start at $20,000.

How much does it cost to fix a website?

Website fixes start at $250. We check your site first and tell you what’s wrong and the exact price before any work begins.

Are there monthly fees after launch?

Only if you want ongoing help. Care plans start at $49 a month for hosting, security, backups and small edits. Domains and some third-party tools have their own yearly fees.

How do payments work?

A 50% deposit starts your project and the balance is due at launch. Prices are fixed in your proposal, and you own your website once it is paid in full.
