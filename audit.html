    Is the website audit really free?

    Yes. You see your overall score and top issues straight away. Leave your email to unlock the full report with every check, a mobile screenshot and a step-by-step fix plan.

    Yes. The audit inspects the public page, so it works on any platform, and it tells you which platform it detected.
