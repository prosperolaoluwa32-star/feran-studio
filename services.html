Questions

Before you ask.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, the platform and the features you need. After a free consultation we send a fixed quote, so you know the full price before we start.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. We agree on a timeline in your proposal and keep you updated at every step.

Which platform should I choose?

Shopify if you sell products. WordPress if you publish a lot of content. Wix or Webflow if you want to make updates yourself. Custom code when you want something fully unique and fast. We’ll recommend one on our call.

Do you work with clients in the U.S.?

Yes. Our team works remotely and serves clients across the United States and on five continents. We schedule calls in your time zone.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. We build on platforms you can manage and walk you through how to make updates.

Can you fix or update my existing website?

Yes. We repair and update websites on WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Webflow and custom code, whether a form or booking isn’t working, pages won’t load, the site looks wrong on phones, or it simply needs a refresh. Fixes start at $250.

Can you help my website show up on Google?

Yes. We get your site indexed, set up Google Search Console, Analytics and your Google Business Profile, and optimise your pages for the searches your customers make. SEO setup starts at $600.

Do you build mobile apps and videos too?

Yes. We design and develop iOS and Android apps, and produce short video ads and promos for social media.
